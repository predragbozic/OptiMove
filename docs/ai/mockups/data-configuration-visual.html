Data configuration — visual proposal

Three layouts for Training load → Data configuration (Variables, Domains & categories, Source fields), shared icons, and dragging variables into categories. Each layout is shown on a computer (scaled) and on a phone (360 px).

Static mockup: not the app, no server, invented names and numbers. The text of the proposal is docs/ai/data-configuration-visual-proposal.md.

Variant A — Board

Domains are a row of cards across the top. The chosen domain's categories are columns, holding their variables as chips. On a computer, the tray on the right holds the variables that are in no category yet, and a chip is dragged onto a category to add it there. On a phone, each category has an Add variables button that opens the picker.

Variant B — Two panes

On the left is one list in three groups that look different: domains (filled tiles), categories in no domain (outlined tiles), and the archived ones, folded. On the right is the chosen item: a domain with its categories, or a category with its variables. On a phone the list and the item are two screens.

Variant C — Home cards

Data configuration opens on cards like the coach's Home: Variables, Domains, Categories and Source fields, each with its count and first rows, and a chevron to open it. Domains and categories are separate screens. On a computer the Categories screen takes the dragged variables.

Shared by every variant

The icon picker, Settings → Icons, the variables list and the Source fields list look the same whichever layout is chosen.

Icons: a sample of 55 icons from Tabler Icons 3.50.0 (outline), MIT License, Copyright (c) 2020-2026 Paweł Kuna. The proposed library would hold about 100, curated by category.
